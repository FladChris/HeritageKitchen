# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Early release of Heritage Kitchen.

- Preserve family recipes with stories, source notes, photo galleries, grouped ingredients and multi-day instructions.
- Find recipes with search, categories and favorites. Scale servings and follow ingredient and step checklists in cooking mode.
- Install the app and sync the full cookbook with photos for offline reading.
- Share recipes as text, links or printable PDFs. Export the whole cookbook as a ZIP with photos or as a PDF.
- Read recipes as a guest. Sign in to comment, rate or edit according to your role.
- Self-host in one container with SQLite or PostgreSQL, automatic migrations, backup and restore scripts, and optional SMTP.
